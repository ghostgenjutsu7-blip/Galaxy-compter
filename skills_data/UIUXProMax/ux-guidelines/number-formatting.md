**No:** 86
**Category:** Content
**Issue:** Number Formatting
**Platform:** All
**Description:** Format large numbers for readability
**Do:** Use thousand separators or abbreviations
**Don't:** Long unformatted numbers
**Code Example Good:** 1.2K or 1,234
**Code Example Bad:** 1234567
**Severity:** Low
